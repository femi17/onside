## Compliance rules (why the copy reads like this)

- **We are a tracker/stats app, not a gambling service.** Never mention odds, stakes, naira
  amounts, winnings, cashouts, bonuses, or bookmaker names in ad copy or creative.
- **No outcome promises.** "Graded in the open" yes; "winning tips", "sure odds", "banker
  loading" no. Nothing that implies guaranteed or likely profit.
- **18+ on every creative and in every description.** Audience targeting 18+ only.
- **No urgency/FOMO gambling tropes** ("don't miss today's games!") — Meta's automated review
  pattern-matches these in this vertical.
- If an ad is rejected: do not resubmit identical creative. Appeal once via Account Quality;
  if upheld, swap the creative/copy pair, never argue the gambling classification in public.
