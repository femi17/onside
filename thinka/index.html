Loota

● Live Flagship

A live, map-based, skill-driven treasure-hunt game. Businesses sponsor real prizes; players scan a QR from a screen in the wild, then navigate the city solving challenges to collect keys. Payments

How we use Paystack.

Thinka Platforms Ltd uses Paystack to securely collect payments inside our products. On Loota, players use Paystack to buy optional in-game items — speed tools like bicycle, motorbike and car rentals, fuel, rest, hints and premium items — that let them move faster during a hunt. Loota is free to play and these purchases never guarantee a win, so paying is always optional. We do not run a lottery, we do not accept stakes, and players do not deposit or withdraw cash.

Card details are never stored on our servers — they are handled entirely by Paystack, a PCI-DSS-certified processor. For the full breakdown of exactly what is sold, how the checkout works, and screenshots of the payment flow, see the Loota page.
