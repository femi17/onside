Payments

How we accept payments.

Across our platforms, Thinka Platforms Ltd accepts payments securely online through Paystack. What a customer pays for depends on the product they are using:

  • Loota — optional in-game items such as speed tools (bicycle, motorbike, car rentals), fuel, rest, hints and premium items. The game is free to play; these purchases are optional and never guarantee a win.
  • Onside — subscription plans that unlock premium analytics features, billed on a recurring basis.
  • Roomy — a Windows PC cleaner: the first clean is free, then a simple one-off unlock or a monthly plan, paid in naira.

Prices are shown in Nigerian Naira before checkout. Card and bank details are entered on Paystack's PCI-DSS-certified checkout and are never stored on our servers — we only receive a transaction reference and status to deliver what was purchased.
